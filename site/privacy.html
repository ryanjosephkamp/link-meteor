Privacy

Link Meteor works entirely inside your browser. It has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sends your links anywhere.

In short

  • Your collections are stored by Chrome on your computer, in storage that belongs to the extension. They are not uploaded, synced or shared by Link Meteor.
  • The extension contains no code that contacts a server, its pages' security policy forbids them from making network requests, and capturing a link never visits its destination.
  • There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no paid tier.
  • Permissions beyond the basics are optional, requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and can be removed in Chrome at any time.

What's stored, and where

Each collection holds its name, notes and tags, and the links you captured. For every link: visible anchor text, accessible label, destination URL, the original href, the source page's address and title, the frame's address, the capture time and batch ID, and your notes and tags.

This is kept in chrome.storage.local, extension storage in your current Chrome profile. Link Meteor does not put it in Chrome Sync. Backups of your computer or Chrome profile are separate from Link Meteor and follow their own settings.

While Chrome is open, Link Meteor also keeps the tab you last opened it from and the latest capture report in session storage, so the side panel and full workbench agree. If you leave out navigation links, the latest capture's left-out links wait there too, so you can still include them, and the card's recent adds are listed so Undo can take one back. Session storage is cleared when Chrome closes, and you can dismiss the report at any time.

Your settings are saved with your collections:

  • the hold key;
  • the sites where hold-key drag runs or never runs;
  • whether you chose to allow all sites;
  • your answer to the welcome card;
  • your export file-name preferences;
  • your theme, and whether it is light, dark or follows the system;
  • what releasing a drag does, and the two capture defaults.

The side panel and full view also keep the last theme and light or dark choice in their own page storage, so they open in it without a flash.

After you restore a backup, the state from just before is kept once in the same local storage, so the restore can be undone. It's replaced by the next restore, and cleared when you discard it or change anything else.

Permissions

Chrome shows you what an extension can do. Here is every permission Link Meteor uses, and why.

PermissionWhenWhy
activeTabAlwaysTemporary access to a page only when you invoke Link Meteor on it with the toolbar, shortcut or right-click menu.
scriptingAlwaysRuns the local selection and capture code on a page you've given it access to.
storageAlwaysKeeps your collections and settings in this browser.
unlimitedStorageAlways, with no promptLets large collections, and the Undo kept after a restore, grow past Chrome's default 10 MB for extension storage. The data stays in this browser.
sidePanelAlwaysShows Link Meteor beside the page you're reading.
contextMenusAlwaysAdds the two Link Meteor items to the right-click menu.
clipboardWriteAlwaysCopies what you choose to the clipboard: tables, URLs, Markdown, rich links, or diagnostics for a bug report. Link Meteor never reads your clipboard.
tabsOptional, when askedLists your open tabs so you can choose which to capture.
Site accessOptional, one site at a timeNeeded to capture tabs other than the one you invoked it on, to capture a page again after its temporary access ended, and for hold-key drag on the sites you choose.
All sitesOptional, only if you choose itOne choice, Allow on all sites: on the welcome card, the Site access switch, or a capture Chrome hid from Link Meteor. Hold-key drag and capture then work on every site except the ones you list under Never on these sites. It is never asked for at install, and declining keeps the one-site-at-a-time route.
tabGroupsOptional, when askedNames a tab group after your collection when you open links as a group. Without it, the tabs still open in an unnamed group.
downloadsOptional, when askedSaves the files behind links you choose, such as PDFs, images and documents, into your Downloads folder, as if you had clicked each link. Chrome calls this "Manage your downloads"; Link Meteor never reads, opens, changes or removes your other downloads.
bookmarksOptional, when askedCreates the bookmark folder you request, or adds links to a folder you choose. To show the folder picker, it reads your bookmark folder names, and it reads the chosen folder's links to skip ones already there. It never changes or removes other bookmarks.

Link Meteor does not request access to your history, cookies, passwords, network traffic or incognito windows, and it never reads your list of downloads. You can review and remove its optional access at any time from Link Meteor's card in chrome://extensions. Turning hold-key drag off for a site stops it there; removing the site's access in Chrome also turns it off. Removing all-sites access, with Link Meteor's Remove Chrome's access or Chrome's own controls, can also remove access to sites you allowed one at a time. Link Meteor then turns hold-key drag off wherever it no longer has access.

Actions that move data

A few actions deliberately take links out of Link Meteor, and only when you choose them:

Copy
Puts the text you chose on your computer's clipboard, where other apps can read it.
Download
Saves a file to your normal downloads folder.
Backup
Saves one file with every collection, link, note, tag and setting to your downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded. Restoring reads a file you choose, shows a preview before anything changes, and treats the file as untrusted: links are checked and unknown fields are dropped.
Bookmarks
Creates bookmarks in Chrome, in a new folder or one you choose. Chrome may sync bookmarks to your other devices, depending on your own Chrome sync settings.
Open in tabs
Visits those links, in tabs, a new window or a tab group, exactly as if you had clicked them, so those sites receive ordinary requests from your browser. Up to 500 at a time, and Link Meteor asks first above 20.

Link Meteor keeps links exactly as captured and never rewrites them. Addresses from private or admin pages can contain sensitive details, so check an export before you share it. Text taken from pages is treated as data: it is escaped in HTML and Markdown exports and protected from being run as formulas in spreadsheet exports.

Deleting your data

Remove individual links, everything in the current view, or every link in a collection, each with undo. You can also delete a whole collection from its details, which can't be undone. Removing Link Meteor from Chrome normally deletes all of its stored data. There's no cloud copy to recover from, so download a backup or export anything you want to keep first.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, advertising, trackers or embedded third-party content. Its fonts, images and scripts are served from the site itself. The interactive demo and the export preview run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere. If you choose a theme from the menu, your choice is kept in this browser’s local storage for this site. It is never sent anywhere.

The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub operates those servers and, like any web host, may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that.

Questions or concerns can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page. This page describes the current development build; the extension hasn't yet been submitted to the Chrome Web Store.
